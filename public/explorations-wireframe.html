⚠ Branching is architecturally invisible — a Condition Branch node looks identical to any other node when collapsed. The "Architect" persona cannot see parallel paths, loop-backs, or exit conditions without expanding every node individually. Complex flows with 8+ nodes become an unscrollable list with no spatial logic.
